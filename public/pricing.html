Common questions
What counts as a "product"?
One product in your Shopify catalog, regardless of how many variants it has. A t-shirt with 10 sizes counts as one product.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from your dashboard at any time. Your translations stay in Shopify — we never delete them on cancellation.
What happens when I hit my limit?
New products above your limit won't be auto-translated. Existing translations are untouched. Upgrade anytime to continue.
Do you support annual billing?
Yes — pay annually and save 20%. Billed once per year. Switch between monthly and annual at renewal.
Which languages are supported?
French, German, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, English, Dutch, and Polish — 8 languages total. Pick the ones that matter for your store, up to your plan's limit.
Is my store data safe?
We only access product data — titles, descriptions, metadata. Never orders, customers, or payment information.
