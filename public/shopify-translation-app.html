What is a Shopify translation app — and why most get it wrong

A Shopify translation app connects to your store and converts your product titles, descriptions, and metadata into other languages so international customers can shop in their own language. Simple concept. But the execution is where most tools fail merchants.

Most translation apps do one thing: run your text through a generic translation engine and push it back to Shopify. The result reads like a machine wrote it — because a machine did. Your "premium leather bag" becomes "superior leather sack" in French. Your brand voice disappears. Your conversion rate in foreign markets stays flat.

Getoify approaches Shopify localization differently. Instead of translating words, it localizes your catalog — applying your brand tone, respecting your glossary, generating SEO metadata per language, and keeping everything synchronized as your store evolves.

The core difference: translation tools convert words. Getoify converts your catalog into a local shopping experience — in every market you sell in.

What Getoify does that other Shopify translation apps don't

1. Brand voice is built in, not bolted on

Before translating a single product, Getoify asks you to define your tone — formal or conversational, luxury or direct — and your brand glossary. Every translation, in every language, respects those parameters. Your forbidden words stay out. Your brand terms stay consistent. "Checkout" is always "checkout", not "basket" or "cart".

This matters because your brand is not just what you sell — it's how you sound. Losing that in translation is losing the thing that makes customers choose you over a cheaper alternative.

2. AI description generation from zero

Many Shopify stores launch with products that have no description at all — just a title and a price. Other translation apps skip these entirely. Getoify generates a complete, SEO-optimized product description from the product title alone, then translates it into all five languages. You go from a blank product page to a fully localized listing without writing a single word.

This is particularly valuable for merchants who import products from suppliers, use dropshipping, or launch large catalogs quickly. Products that had no description yesterday have five localized versions today.

3. SEO included — not optional

Ranking on Google in French, German, or Spanish requires more than translated product text. It requires correctly set meta_title, meta_description, and hreflang tags in each language. Getoify generates all of these automatically for every product, in every language. You don't need a separate SEO app. You don't need to manually write 5,000 meta descriptions. It's done.

4. Auto-sync with Shopify webhooks

Most translation apps require you to manually trigger a re-translation when you update a product. Getoify listens to Shopify's native webhook system. When you add a product, it's localized within 2 minutes. When you update a product title or description, the translations update automatically across all languages. Your catalog is always current — without you touching anything.

5. Direct Shopify Translations API integration

Getoify writes translations directly to Shopify's native Translations API — the same system Shopify uses internally. This means your translations work with Shopify Markets, Shopify Translate & Adapt, and any other native localization feature. No workarounds, no theme edits, no custom code. It just works.

Which languages does Getoify support?

Getoify currently supports five languages that cover the major European e-commerce markets: French, German, Italian, Spanish, and Portuguese. These five languages together cover over 400 million potential customers across the EU and Latin America — the core expansion target for most English-speaking and French-speaking Shopify merchants.

Each language is handled independently with its own brand voice calibration, its own glossary application, and its own SEO metadata generation. A German translation is not a French translation run through a second engine — it's built from scratch for the German market.
