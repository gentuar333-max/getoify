The short version

Langify is a solid, established Shopify translation tool. It does what it says: it translates your store content into other languages, gives you a manual editor to tweak things, and integrates cleanly with most Shopify themes. For merchants who want full manual control over every translated word, it's a reasonable choice.

Getoify is built for merchants who want the opposite: maximum automation, minimum manual work. It translates, generates missing descriptions with AI, applies your brand voice, handles SEO metadata, and keeps everything synchronized automatically. For high-volume stores and merchants who don't want to manage translations manually, Getoify is the better fit.

The key question: do you want to manage translations manually, or do you want them handled automatically with your brand voice preserved? Your answer determines which tool is right for you.

Where Getoify wins

AI description generation — Langify cannot do this

This is the clearest functional difference between the two apps. When a product has no description — just a title and a price — Langify has nothing to translate. It skips those products entirely, leaving blank product pages in your target-language storefronts.

Getoify generates a complete product description from the title alone using Claude AI, then translates it into all five languages. A merchant importing 500 products from a supplier with no descriptions goes from 500 blank pages to 500 fully localized listings — without writing a single word.

Brand voice is built in from the start

Langify translates your existing text. If your existing text is average, the translation will be average. There is no mechanism in Langify to apply a consistent tone, protect specific terms, or ensure that the output sounds like your brand rather than a generic translation.

Getoify starts from your brand identity. Before translating anything, you define your tone — formal, conversational, luxury, technical — and your glossary of protected terms. Every translation, in every language, is generated within those parameters. The result sounds like you wrote it — because the AI was instructed to write like you.

Auto-sync happens automatically, not on a schedule

In Langify, when you update a product, you need to manually trigger a re-translation or wait for a scheduled sync. For stores that update inventory frequently, this creates lag — customers in target-language markets see outdated content.

Getoify uses Shopify's native webhook system. When you update a product title or description, Getoify detects the change and re-translates all language versions within minutes. There is no manual step, no scheduled job, no lag. Your multilingual store is always current.

Where Langify wins

More languages

Langify supports all languages available in Shopify. Getoify currently supports five: French, German, Italian, Spanish, and Portuguese. If you need Japanese, Arabic, Korean, or other languages not in Getoify's current set, Langify covers those markets. Getoify is expanding its language support, but today Langify has broader coverage.

Manual editor for fine-grained control

Langify's manual translation editor is mature and feature-complete. If you want to review and adjust every translated field before publishing, Langify gives you that workflow. Getoify's dashboard shows translation status and lets you view results, but it's built around trust in the AI output rather than manual review. For merchants who want to approve every translation, Langify is the better fit.

Pricing comparison

Langify starts at $17.50/month for unlimited words and languages. Getoify starts at $19/month for 500 products and 3 languages, scaling to $99/month for 5,000 products. If you have a very large catalog and need all languages, Langify's flat-rate model may be more economical. For stores in the 500–5,000 product range focused on European markets, Getoify's pricing is comparable.

The more relevant cost comparison is the value difference: Getoify includes AI generation, brand voice, and SEO automation that would require additional tools alongside Langify. The effective cost of a comparable feature set often makes Getoify the more economical choice. See Getoify pricing →
